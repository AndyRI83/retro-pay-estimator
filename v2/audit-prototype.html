What RetroCalc has been tested on

RetroCalc was built by taking apart real University Campus retro statements line by line and checking the math week by week. The current test cases include Baylor and non-Baylor OT, holidays, step changes, differentials, leave, and several other pay types.

Your pay history may include something RetroCalc has not seen yet. If that happens, it should tell you what it cannot check instead of guessing.

Pay situations we still want more examples of
  • On-call / callback
  • Step increase during the retro period
  • Non-Baylor overtime
  • Unfamiliar or department-specific pay
  • Statements that seem to be missing older pay

Want to help? Contact Andrew privately. Your name and other identifying information are not needed and may be redacted. Sharing a statement for testing is optional and separate from using RetroCalc.

What RetroCalc checks

  • Whether the hours and pay shown on this statement were recalculated correctly under the pay rules RetroCalc has validated.
  • Whether all of the pay lines RetroCalc reads add back to the gross pay printed on the statement.

What this PDF cannot prove by itself

  • Whether every hour you actually worked was recorded correctly in the first place.
  • Whether your taxes were withheld correctly.
  • Whether insurance or other benefit deductions were calculated correctly.
