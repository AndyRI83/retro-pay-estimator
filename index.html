How it works

For Apr 2025–Mar 2026, we apply the 3.25% increase to the prior rate for the step you selected. For Apr–Aug 2026, we apply both increases to the prior rate for the step selected for that date range.

Regular, Baylor, and PTO hours use the straight-time rate difference. Overtime uses a 1.5× multiplier. For Baylor, enter only the additional 6 or 8 Baylor Plan hours paid separately, not the regular 24 or 36 worked hours.

Calculations carry full precision and round only displayed currency to cents.

Important input notes

  • Baylor hours: enter only the additional Baylor Plan hours paid separately.

  • Differentials: fixed-dollar shift, weekend, resource, and preceptor differentials are not included.
Why isn’t it simply 6.25%?

Because the second raise applies to a wage that already includes the first raise. The cumulative increase is 6.3475%.

1.0325 × 1.03 = 1.063475
1.063475 − 1 = 0.063475
Cumulative increase = 6.3475%

Accuracy basis

Prior rates match the University Campus Appendix B scale effective 4/7/2024. Each date range uses the prior rate for the wage step selected for that range. The estimator models the provided 3.25% and 3.00% increases using full-precision compounding.
