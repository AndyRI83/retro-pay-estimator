Pay stub terms

This is the full library of Workday pay terms the checker currently knows about. It is the same for everyone and will grow as new pay codes are validated.

Type the words Workday shows on your statement, or just type what you are looking for.

Example: if you see Charge Pay and do not know what it means, type Charge Pay. You can also search plain words like holiday or sick.

Regular pay and paid time off

Regular PayYour normal hourly pay
✓

These are your ordinary paid work hours. The retro payment adds the difference between what those hours were paid at originally and what the new wage scale says they should have been paid at.

Vacation PayPaid vacation hours
✓

Vacation hours are paid using your hourly wage. If your wage rate went up, those old vacation hours have to be corrected too.

Sick Pay / MA SickPaid sick hours
✓

These are paid sick hours. The retro check corrects the hourly wage used to pay those hours.

Personal timeUnscheduled Personal / Scheduled Personal
✓

Paid personal-time hours. If they were paid at your hourly wage, the retro check corrects them to the new wage rate.

BereavementPaid bereavement leave
✓

Paid bereavement hours. They follow your hourly wage, so the retro check corrects them when the wage scale changes.

Meeting / Paid TrainingPaid time for a meeting or training
✓

Paid hours for work meetings or training. The retro check corrects the hourly wage used for those hours.

Baylor TimeExtra Baylor-plan pay
✓

This is the extra pay created by the Baylor schedule. Because it is tied to the base wage, a wage increase changes the Baylor amount too.

Baylor pay can also make the OT rate a little higher in a week where you worked overtime.

Overtime and holidays

Overtime PayOvertime hours
✓

This is pay for overtime hours.

The OT rate can change from week to week because some extra pay earned that week can raise the OT rate. That is why the checker rebuilds OT one week at a time.

See the OT math

Holiday Worked / Holiday Worked OT / Holiday Overtime / Holiday Worked 0.5Different pieces of pay for working a major holiday
✓

One holiday shift can show up as several Workday lines. Together, those lines build the extra holiday pay and any overtime that also applies.

You do not need to add them yourself. The checker reads the pieces and rebuilds the week.

Holiday / Scheduled Holiday / Cash Out HolidayHoliday hours paid to you
✓

These are holiday hours paid at your wage rate, including holiday time you used or cashed out. The retro check corrects the wage used for those hours.

Extra pay added to certain hours

Night Shift DifferentialExtra pay for qualifying night hours
◷ Separate

Before the increase: $5.00/hour. New rate: $5.50/hour.

The 50-cent increase goes back to April 2025, but it is being handled separately from the September 3 retro payment. The payment date has not been confirmed.

Night pay can also make your OT rate higher in weeks where you worked overtime.

Charge PayThis is the Workday name we have seen for Resource / Flow pay
◷ Separate

Plain English: extra hourly pay for working Resource or Flow.

Before the increase: $3.50/hour. New rate: $3.75/hour.

The 25-cent increase goes back to April 2025, but it is being handled separately from the September 3 retro payment. The payment date has not been confirmed.

Resource / Flow pay can also make your OT rate higher in a week where you worked overtime.

Evening ShiftExtra pay for qualifying evening hours
✓

The rate stayed at $3.00/hour. There is no new retro increase to the evening differential itself.

Workday may still show evening lines while it redoes a week's OT math.

Weekend DifferentialExtra pay for qualifying weekend hours
✓

The rate stayed at $3.00/hour. Weekend pay can still make the OT rate higher in a week with overtime.

PreceptorExtra pay for precepting
✓

Preceptor pay is $2.50/hour. That rate did not change as part of this retro wage increase.

Preceptor pay can still make the OT rate higher in a week with overtime.

Other things you may see

Certification BonusOne-time $500 payment
✓

This is a one-time $500 payment for an eligible certification. That's it.

It is not hourly pay, it is not tied to your wage step, and it does not change your overtime rate. The wage raises did not change the $500 amount.

Workday may print strange-looking values in the Hours or Rate columns beside the bonus. Ignore them. The payment itself is $500.

Because the bonus amount did not change, it adds $0 to this retro wage payment.

Workers Compensation / Industrial AccidentCurrent workers' compensation pay
✓

This can appear on the same PDF as old retro adjustments even though it belongs to the current check. The checker separates it before calculating the retro amount.

On-call / callbackExact Workday labels still being collected

We have not yet seen enough real Workday call/callback examples to safely recognize every version of those lines or every OT effect. If the checker finds one, it should tell you that part cannot be fully checked instead of guessing.
