IMPORTANT QA DEFECT FOUND AND FIXED
During the hostile pre-release review, the Night & Resource audit could still return a reassuring result if a PDF was truncated in a way that removed a matched adjustment pair. The parsed lines still reconciled internally, but they no longer reconciled to Workday's printed gross.

Fixes now in this candidate:
- Night & Resource audit fails closed when parsed pay lines do not reconcile to the printed gross.
- A failed statement-integrity check no longer exposes a definitive reconstructed Night & Resource total.
- Old-rate/new-rate differential hours are checked for matched historical pairs.
- Unknown historical pay types, including unresolved Call / callback pay, prevent a fully verified result instead of being silently ignored.
- Re-uploading a second PDF in the same browser session no longer loses the live result element used by the UI.
- Combined tax/deduction comparison refreshes on a second analysis instead of retaining stale values.
