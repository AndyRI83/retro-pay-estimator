Anonymous usage statistics

Retro Pay Checker uses GoatCounter for simple aggregate traffic and feature-use counts. This helps answer questions like: How many people visited the site? Which pages were used? How many Part 1, Part 2, or two-PDF checks were completed?

What analytics can include: the page path (for example, /wage-scale.html), short event names such as part2-check-completed or report-shared, and GoatCounter's aggregate browser/device statistics such as browser, operating system, language, approximate location, and screen width.
What analytics does not include: your PDF, filename, name, employee ID, pay amounts, pay codes, statement dates, audit result, or anything extracted from your pay stub.

Retro Pay Checker strips query strings from the page path sent to analytics and does not intentionally send the referring page. GoatCounter says it does not store IP addresses, the full User-Agent header, cookies, localStorage identifiers, or other persistent tracking IDs. Analytics is optional plumbing: if it is blocked or unavailable, the checker still works.

What happens when I close the page?

The checker does not save your pay history. Refreshing or closing the page clears the statement from the checker.

Technical detail: the site downloads a pinned PDF-reading library from jsDelivr. The paycheck PDF itself is not sent to jsDelivr by this tool.

✓ What this can check

  • Whether the pay lines on a PDF add up to Workday's gross amount.
  • Whether known pay types were recalculated using the expected wage or differential rate.
  • Overtime, week by week, when the needed pay types are understood.
  • Part 1, Part 2, or both statements together.
  • What taxes Workday withheld and what dated benefit-deduction adjustments appear on the statement.

× What the PDFs cannot prove

  • Whether every hour you actually worked was entered correctly before payroll ran.
  • Whether a differential was completely missing from the original time record.
  • Whether your final income-tax bill or refund will be correct.
  • Whether an insurance-plan premium adjustment is correct under the plan formula.
  • Hours or earnings that do not appear in either statement.

Reports and sharing

Generated reports are created in your browser. Using Save or send asks your device to open its normal sharing options when supported. If you choose Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Files, or another destination, that transfer is handled by your device and the app you choose.

What if the checker sees something unfamiliar?

It stops or marks that part as uncertain. It is deliberately designed to say “we can't fully check this” instead of turning an unfamiliar payroll line into a fake underpayment.
