Your pay stub stays on your device

When you choose a PDF, the checker reads it inside your web browser. The PDF is not uploaded to Andrew, a Retro Pay Checker server, or a database.

What happens when I close the page?

The checker does not save your pay history. Refreshing or closing the page clears the statement from the checker.

Technical detail: the site currently downloads a pinned PDF-reading library from jsDelivr. The paycheck PDF itself is not sent to jsDelivr by this tool.

✓ What this can check

  • Whether the pay lines on the PDF add up to Workday's gross amount.
  • Whether known pay types were recalculated using the expected wage rate.
  • Overtime, week by week, when the needed pay types are understood.
  • Step-rate changes that are visible on the statement.

× What one PDF cannot prove

  • Whether every hour you actually worked was entered correctly before payroll ran.
  • Whether a differential was completely missing from the original time record.
  • Whether taxes or insurance deductions were calculated correctly.
  • The final Night / Resource payment when older hours are not shown.

What if the checker sees something unfamiliar?

It stops or marks that part as uncertain. It is deliberately designed to say “we can't fully check this” instead of turning an unfamiliar payroll line into a fake underpayment.
